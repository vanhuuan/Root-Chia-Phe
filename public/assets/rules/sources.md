## Marquise de Cat tutorial
- `cat-warrior.png`, `cat-keep.png`, `cat-sawmill.png`: faithful component crops from Leder Games' Root Learn to Play (2020), pages 24 and 11.
- `cat-recruiter.png`: recruiter symbol cropped from the Marquise faction board's empty first recruiter space; `cat-building-tracks.png`: the building cost/VP/draw tracks cropped from the same official board as reproduced in the Learn to Play PDF. No AI-generated pieces.

## Eyrie Dynasties tutorial
- `eyrie-roost.png`: faithful printed roost crop, Learning to Play p7, same official PDF source above.
- `leader-builder.webp`, `leader-charismatic.webp`, `leader-commander.webp`, `leader-despot.webp`: authentic full leader cards from Leder Card Library, https://ledercards.netlify.app/cards/root/en-US/bird-builder.webp (and bird-charismatic.webp, bird-commander.webp, bird-despot.webp at the same directory).
- `eyrie-sword-card.webp`: https://ledercards.netlify.app/cards/root/en-US/card-sword.webp ; `eyrie-sword-item.png`: item crop from that card. Mouse suit; crafting cost two Fox; printed reward sword and 2 VP (Eyrie normally receive only 1 VP due to Disdain for Trade; Builder ignores it).
- Decree tiles and numeric roost track are teaching diagrams, labeled as such, using authentic suit icons. They are not replicas of printed cards or the full faction board. Loyal Viziers are identified by name and Bird suit without fabricated card artwork.
- Rules checked against official Law of Root (Oct 2025), section 7: https://rules.ledergames.com/?product=root&locale=en-US
